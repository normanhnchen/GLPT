# BSDF

A BSDF, or bidirectional scattering distribution function, describes how light scatters off a surface
given an incident and outgoing ray direction, and the surface's properties.
It contains two parts:

- BRDF (Bidirectional Reflectance Distribution Function): Describes light reflected into the upper hemisphere
- BTDF (Bidirectional Transmittance Distribution Function): Describes light transmitted through the surface into the lower hemisphere

## Microfacet Models

Microfacet models are based on the idea that surfaces are collections of microscopic mirrors called *microfacets*. The distribution and interaction of these microfacets are defined by three core components:

- **Normal Distribution Function ($D$)**: Describes the statistical alignment of microfacet normals $\omega_m$ relative to the surface normal. See the [Trowbridge-Reitz GGX function](5.4_Trowbridge-Reitz_GGX.md)

- **Geometry / Masking-Shadowing Function ($G$)**: Accounts for microfacets blocking each other from the light source (shadowing) or the incident ray direction (masking). See the simplified [Schlick-GGX approximation](5.5_Schlick-GGX_Approximation.md) or the accurate [height-correlated Smith](5.6_Height-Correlated_Smith.md).

- **Fresnel ($F$)**: Calculates the amount of reflected light based on the incident angle relative to the microfacet normal $\omega_m$. See the [Fresnel-Schlick approximation](5.7_Fresnel-Schlick_Approximation.md).

Combining $D$, $G$ and $F$, we can statistically approximate how light scatters off of a surface.

## Sampling $\omega_i$.

To calculate the BSDF, we require an outgoing ray direction $\omega_i$. A material's scattering behavior is modeled by multiple components called *lobes*. To maintain performance and mathematical accuracy, we sample only one lobe at a time:

- Diffuse Lobe: Samples $\omega_i$ for a completely matte (Lambertian) surface. See [cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling](5.3_Cosine-Weighted_Hemisphere_Sampling.md).
- Specular Lobe: Samples $\omega_i$ for specular reflection. See [GGX VNDF importance sampling](5.8_GGX_VNDF_Importance_Sampling.md).
- Transmission Lobe: Samples $\omega_i$ for dielectric refraction. See [microfacet transmission](5.9_Microfacet_Transmission.md).

To select which branch, or lobe to take, they are stochastically selected depending on the surface's properties. See the section on [lobe selection](5.10_Lobe_Selection.md).
